## Overview

QeEdu is an AI-native agent platform for higher education workflows. It is designed as a lightweight AI assistance layer for teachers, students and campus staff, not as a replacement for existing campus systems.

## Editions

- Community: open-source self-hosted foundation.
- Cloud: hosted product experience at `https://cloud.qeedu.tech`.
- Education: private deployment and campus customization.
